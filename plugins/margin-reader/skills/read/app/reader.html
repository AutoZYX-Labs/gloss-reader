Direct intervention makes Δe the simulator-defined effect of removal, but the qualification analysis shows that it is difficult to recover from the compact pre-action observation.
Claude

In short: Δe is the true effect of removing an object, but a model can't reliably predict it from what the robot sees beforehand.

Because the simulator actually removes each object and measures the change, Δe is causally correct by construction. The catch is learnability: the compact observation available before acting doesn't carry enough information to estimate it well.
